## 🌱 Currently building: HazelCard

> **Technology for better outcomes for care leavers.**

**HazelCard** is an evolving platform designed to connect **care-experienced young people, personal advisers, local authorities and opportunity partners** through one joined-up digital experience.

| Product area | What we're building |
| --- | --- |
| 🧭 **Personalised growth journeys** | Profiles, skills, aspirations, goals and actionable development plans |
| 🤖 **AI-assisted guidance** | Conversational support to help surface strengths, identify goals and suggest next steps for adviser review |
| 🏛️ **Council Local Offers** | Council-managed pages presenting local support, services and opportunities, with configurable content and presentation |
| 🤝 **Partner opportunities** | Access to relevant education, training, employment and partner offers |
| 📋 **Professional dashboards** | Caseload visibility, tasks, referrals, support-plan tracking and collaboration for personal advisers and teams |
| 🔐 **Responsible platform engineering** | Role-based access, environment separation, auditability and attention to privacy and security |

**My focus:** combining full-stack architecture, practical AI integration, product workflows and scalable cloud services to make complex support systems easier to use.

*HazelCard is ongoing work; features described here represent the product direction and active development, not a claim that every feature is publicly released.*

## 🚀 Selected projects & engineering work

### 🔍 Intelligent OCR & document understanding
**PyTorch · Computer Vision · Transformers · FastAPI**

- Developed a modular pipeline for text detection, image enhancement and recognition.
- Worked with CRAFT detection, diffusion-based image enhancement, CRNN and transformer-based recognition.
- Exposed model workflows through APIs and explored containerised inference.
- Experimental recognition results: **CER ≈ 0.13** and **WER ≈ 0.19**.

### 🧬 Bio-inspired spiking neural network image encoder
**MSc research · NEST · Python · Neuromorphic Computing**

- Explored Poisson spike encoding and image reconstruction using spiking neural networks.
- Compared **Time-to-First-Spike** and rate-based decoding strategies.
- Evaluated image quality using **PSNR** and **SSIM**.

### 🎙️ Automatic speech recognition
**PyTorch · CNN-BiLSTM-CTC · LibriSpeech**

- Built an experimental end-to-end ASR architecture using log-Mel features.
- Explored speech sequence modelling, CTC training and recognition evaluation.

### 📦 Camera-based logistics platform
**Computer Vision · OpenCV · Full-Stack Engineering**

- Built camera-assisted shipment identification and package dimensioning workflows.
- Developed backend APIs, database structures, operator dashboards and reporting.
- Connected image processing to operational, auditing and pricing workflows.

### ⚙️ Scalable queue management architecture
**PostgreSQL · Redis · Kafka · Azure**

- Designed transaction-safe queue processing, state transitions and idempotent operations.
- Explored Redis-backed real-time updates and event-driven scaling.
- Prioritised durability, observability and reliable concurrent processing.
